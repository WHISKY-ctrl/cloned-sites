How a project runs.

The same five stages, every time — so you always know what's next.

01

Discovery

We start with your goals, constraints, and existing systems — not a template. Scope, budget range, and timeline get defined before any design work starts.

02

Design

Architecture and interface design happen together, so what we design is what we can actually build within budget and timeline.

03

Build

Development in short, visible cycles. You see working software regularly, not a single reveal at the end.

04

Launch

Deployment, monitoring, and a handoff walkthrough — so your team understands what's running and why.

05

Support

Post-launch support and a direct line to the engineers who built it, not a ticket queue starting from zero.
